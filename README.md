# 🍔 toddelicious.truck — Official Food Truck Website

A high-converting, modern, and responsive website crafted for **toddelicious.truck**, inspired directly by the vibrant, energetic food-pop visual aesthetic.

---

## 🌟 Brand Highlights & Links
- **Brand Name**: `toddelicious.truck` (Toddelicious Food Truck)
- **Instagram Page**: [@toddelicious.truck](https://www.instagram.com/toddelicious.truck?exin=MWNneTc4MjVpZD5aA==)
- **Specialty**: 100% Halal Grass-Fed Smashed Angus Burgers, Loaded Dirty Truck Fries, Gourmet Shakes & Scratch Sauces

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Faithful Hero Design (Mockup-Inspired)**:
   - Radiant emerald/lime food-pop gradient background (`#2bd45e` to `#146132`)
   - Giant bold watermark typography: **"FLAVOR POP"**
   - Floating double smash burger with interactive hotspot annotations
   - "Choose your flavor crafted your way" callout with connecting pointer line
   - Center dark-green capsule button: **ORDER NOW**
   - Bottom curved white sheet with:
     - **Notepad Clipboard Card**: "Place your order" with burger illustration and "ORDER YOUR BURGER" link
     - **Brand Slogan**: *"Premium burgers made for those who crave more than just food."*
     - **Stats Card**: *"100K+ Happy bites delivered"* with foodie customer avatar circles and 4.9★ rating
   - Top Navigation with brand logo, links, and pill-shaped **VIEW MENU** button

2. **Live Truck Tracker & Weekly Radar**:
   - Real-time "OPEN / CLOSED" badge and operating hours
   - Today's live location with address and "Get Directions" Google Maps link
   - Day-by-day weekly schedule for Norfolk, waterfront marina, tech hubs, and markets

3. **Interactive Menu & Category Filters**:
   - Categories: Smash Burgers, Loaded Fries, Crispy Chicken, Shakes, House Dips
   - Dietary badges (Halal, Vegetarian, Gluten-Free)
   - Real-time "+ Add to Bag" with dynamic fly-to-bag notifications

4. **Working Slide-Over Bag & Order Drawer**:
   - Subtotal, packaging & tax calculation
   - Promo code engine (try `TODD10` for 10% off)
   - Choice of "🚚 Truck Window Pickup" or "🚗 Curbside"
   - Simulated checkout modal generating a digital order receipt with ticket ID (e.g. `#TD-8942`)

5. **Instagram Social Wall**:
   - Prominently showcases the official handle: `@toddelicious.truck`
   - Direct button to follow on Instagram
   - Responsive photo grid with likes and comments overlay

6. **Event Catering & Private Food Truck Hire**:
   - Interactive guest count slider (30 to 400+ guests)
   - Dynamic real-time quote estimator
   - Inquiry form with instant confirmation

7. **Zero-Dependency Local Server**:
   - Works immediately by opening `index.html` in any web browser
   - Or run `npm start` / `node server.js` to serve on `http://localhost:3000`
